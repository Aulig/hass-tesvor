# Tesvor Home

Control vacuums registered in the Tesvor Home app from Home Assistant.

## Status

Tesvor Home login for a German (`+49`) account, account discovery, device discovery, and dynamic DP-schema retrieval have been validated. Physical controls and map-changing operations are implemented but remain experimental until they have been tested on more Tesvor hardware.

## Highlights

- Standard vacuum controls
- Live map and map camera entities
- Room and rectangular zone cleaning
- Model-dependent suction, water, and cleaning-mode selects
- Cloud maps and cleaning-history maps

See the [repository documentation](https://github.com/Aulig/hass-tesvor) for installation instructions, map-button behavior, limitations, and troubleshooting.
